## 5.0.1: find out why the accurate engine is skipped

Reports from a heavy modpack showed every hand previewed by the old engine (`engine=legacy`), with no reason recorded. This release fixes a likely cause and makes any remaining cause visible.

- The state snapshot no longer follows card definitions and mod objects, and stops at a fixed depth, so large static mod data cannot exhaust its table budget.
- Every reason the accurate engine is skipped is now appended to the audit log line as `why=...` and written once to the Lovely log as `[DVPreview] accurate engine not used: ...`.
- Errors while checking readiness or comparing state are caught and reported instead of aborting silently.
