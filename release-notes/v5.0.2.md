## 5.0.2: audit log gaps and previews during a real hand

- The accuracy audit no longer loses its record of what was previewed. The preview's own hidden test plays used to overwrite that record, so some real hands were never logged.
- A preview no longer starts a hidden test play while a real hand is on its way to being scored. The block ends once the hand reaches scoring (or after 20 seconds as a safety).
- Tests added for both cases.
