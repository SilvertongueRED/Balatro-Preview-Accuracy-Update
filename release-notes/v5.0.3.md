## 5.0.3: preview stays put while the hand is played

When you pressed Play, the preview was recalculated as the played cards left your hand and briefly showed a wrong number (for example 749 instead of 6,713), even though the final score was right.

- While a real hand is being played, nothing is re-simulated any more. The preview stays as it was until the hand scores, then resets as before.
- This applies to both engines, so the old engine can no longer show a wrong number mid-play either.
